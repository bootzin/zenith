Start here

Zenith makes Zen work more like a tiling desktop (i3, Hyprland): most things by keyboard, pages as tiles beside each other, a bar like waybar's. Every part can be turned off in the settings; nothing here is needed to use Zen as it is.

Most of Zenith's keys use Alt, so they don't clash with the page's. Four are worth learning first:

Alt+WGo to any tabA few letters of its title or site, then Enter.
Alt+DOpen a site or searchAn address, a search, or one of your bookmarks.
Alt+Shift+/Every key, on one sheetAny key closes it.
Alt+Shift+ZChange anythingZenith's settings over the page, with a search.

Find and open

The switcher (Alt+W) and the launcher (Alt+D) are one list with modes, like rofi's: tabs, open, spaces, commands, a calculator and emoji. Ctrl+Tab moves between the modes, or click one at the top.

Type : for commands. A few:

:timer 10m tea
A countdown on the page's edge
:pomo
A pomodoro (:pomo 25 5 4)
:appearance dark
Pages dark, light or automatic
:set
Find a setting and change it
:peek
Look something up in a Peek
:help tiles
This page, at a topic
:Every commandThe switcher with : typed.

Links

A link can open in a Peek (a small window over the page that stays while you work), in Zen's Glance, or as a tile. Which key does what is yours to choose: Settings › Shell › Clicking a link with keys.

Alt+;
Letters on every link: type them to follow it (no mouse)
Alt+Shift+;
The same, in a new tab
Alt+Q
Peek: look something up and stay where you are
Alt+QA PeekType a search or an address.

The bar and the Dash

A bar like waybar's, on any edge of the window: spaces, the page's title, the clock, media, downloads, timers, and your tabs if you want them there. Each edge has its own bar (or none), and a bar can hide until the pointer reaches its edge. If that's too many choices, a layout sets them all at once: Settings › Bar.

A top bar
Spaces, the tab you're on, the clock; tabs stay in the sidebar
Top and bottom
Status at the top, this space's tabs at the bottom; no sidebar
A taskbar
One bar at the bottom with tabs and status
A rail
Tabs and status in a slim bar on the left, instead of the sidebar
Minimal
Tabs and status at the top; the sidebar on hover

The Dash (Alt+N, or a click on the bar's clock) has a calendar, the weather, what's playing, your timers, quick toggles and what the browser is using.

Alt+NThe DashAny key or a click away closes it.

Your rice file

Your whole setup can live in one file, ~/.config/zenith/zenith.conf, in Hyprland's config language: palette, bars, layouts, keys. Keep it in your dotfiles, share it; Alt+Shift+C reads it again. Settings › Shell › The rice file writes yours.

palette = nord
Any setting: section { name = value }, or section:name = value
bar { top { modules = … } }
A bar and what it shows: spaces mode | tabs | clock
bind = $mod SHIFT, H, move-left
A Shell key (the names are Alt+Shift+/'s)
:fetch
Your setup as fastfetch prints a system's, for a screenshot
:fetchYour setup at a glanceabout:zenith-fetch.
Alt+OThe overviewEsc, Alt+O or a click away closes it.

Colours and wallpaper

One palette colours the whole browser: the bar, the sidebar, popups, the spaces. Pick a named one (Catppuccin, Gruvbox, Nord, Rosé Pine and more), or let it follow something, as a Linux rice does:

From your wallpaper
Colours picked from the window's wallpaper, again whenever it changes
pywal
Your ~/.cache/wal/colors.json, followed when it changes
wallust
~/.cache/wallust/colors.json (a wallust template that writes pywal's format)

The window's wallpaper can be your desktop's or a picture of your own: Settings › Look.

⚙Choose a paletteSettings › Look › Colour and shape.
